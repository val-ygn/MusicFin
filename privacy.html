Introduction

The MusicFin application for Android and WearOS allows you to stream your music library stored on your personal Jellyfin Server. This page explains what we collect and how we process your personal data.

Collected Data

Please note that all of your data used within the application (URL, music played, settings) is only stored on your own Jellyfin server and inside your application. No application data is collected or stored by us.

Data Usage

None of your data stored on your device leaves this application or your Jellyfin server. An exception is made for website cookies, which are used purely for analytical purposes (number of visits, language, etc.).

Cookies

Stored cookies are only used to help us analyze website traffic and preferences (number of visits, language, etc.).

Third-party Services

The only third-party services used are Netlify for hosting the website, and your own Jellyfin server for your music library.
